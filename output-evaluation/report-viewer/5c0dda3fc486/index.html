Scientific Report: Exoplanet Transit Observation of HD 189733 b

Observation Summary

The transit of exoplanet HD 189733 b was observed on 2026-08-05 using the MicroObservatory telescope (LCOGT node at McDonald Observatory). The data were captured using the sq41 instrument with a V-band filter. The images have a pixel scale of 0.74 arcsec/pixel. Individual exposures were 1.5 seconds, resulting in a high-cadence dataset of 300 FITS images.

Data Reduction

Data reduction was performed using the EXOTIC pipeline. Calibration frames (Flats, Darks, Biases) were not provided in the dataset, and the pipeline proceeded without them. Target and comparison stars were identified visually and then refined via plate solving, which corrected the target star coordinates to [1213, 1190]. Aperture photometry was conducted using an optimal aperture of 3.32 and an optimal annulus of 26.78. Detrending was applied using airmass coefficients (a1 = 1.42 +/- 0.27, a2 = -0.4 +/- 1.38) to remove atmospheric effects.

Transit Results

The transit was modeled using the UltraNest sampler. The resulting fitted parameters are:

  • Mid-Transit Time: 2461256.8537 +/- 0.0021 BJD_TDB
  • Radius Ratio (Rp/Rs): 0.052 +/- 0.054
  • Transit Depth: 0.27 +/- 0.57 %
  • Orbital Inclination: 85.78 +/- 2.69 degrees
  • Transit Duration: 0.006 +/- 0.013 days

Data Quality and Analysis

The quality of the resulting light curve is poor, as evidenced by a high residual scatter of 17.74%. EXOTIC was unable to determine a "Best Comparison Star" from the selected set, suggesting significant noise or instability in the comparison stars or the target. The recovered radius ratio and transit depth are consistent with zero within their large uncertainties, indicating that a significant transit signal was not clearly recovered from this specific dataset.
