The fitted radius ratio (Rp/R* = 0.002 ± 0.043) is consistent with zero. The literature value is Rp/R* ≈ 0.118 (Bakos et al. 2011), corresponding to a ~1.4% transit depth. No transit was detected.

Data Quality

The observation missed the transit by ~2.1 days. The predicted T₀ was BJD 2461116.593, but observations started at BJD 2461118.712, covering phases 0.63–0.88 (post-transit). The flux values oscillate around 1.0 with ~1% scatter, consistent with photon noise and atmospheric variability. The 0.83% residual scatter indicates acceptable photometric quality for this dataset.

Conclusions

This observation did not capture the HAT-P-36 b transit due to an ephemeris offset of approximately 2.1 days. The transit occurred at BJD 2461116.593, well before the observation window. The photometric precision (0.83% scatter) suggests that a well-timed observation with this configuration could detect the ~1.4% transit. Future observations should use updated ephemerides and begin observations closer to the predicted mid-transit time.
