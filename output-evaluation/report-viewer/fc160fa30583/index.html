Scientific Transit Report: HAT-P-20 b

Observation Summary

  • Target: HAT-P-20 b
  • Telescope: MicroObservatory
  • Instrument: sq30 (CCD)
  • Observation Date: 2025-02-04
  • Filter: SDSS-RP (Sloan r)
  • Exposure Time: 20.0 seconds
  • Cadence: ~20 seconds
  • FITS Dataset: 300 frames

Data Reduction

The dataset was processed using the EXOTIC (EXOplanet Transit Interpretation Code) pipeline.

  1. Calibration: The science frames were processed using standard bias, dark, and flat-field corrections as specified in the FITS headers.
  2. Aperture Photometry: Target and comparison star coordinates were identified visually and validated via FITS intensity peaks. EXOTIC determined that PSF photometry was the optimal method for this dataset.
  3. Comparison Star Selection: Four comparison stars were provided; EXOTIC identified the star at [1046.0, 1081.0] as the best comparison star for minimizing noise.
  4. Transit Fitting: A Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) approach via UltraNest was used to fit the light curve and derive planetary parameters.

Transit Results

The analysis yielded the following planetary parameters:

  • Mid-Transit Time (BJD_TDB): 2460710.79887 $\pm$ 0.00083
  • Transit Depth: 2.27 $\pm$ 0.091 %
  • Radius Ratio ($R_p/R_s$): 0.1505 $\pm$ 0.003
  • Orbital Inclination: 86.75 $\pm$ 0.11$^\circ$
  • Transit Duration: 0.07475 $\pm$ 0.00099 days

Data Quality

The fit quality was high, with a residual scatter of 0.52%. The UltraNest posterior sampling converged successfully, and the resulting parameters are physically plausible for HAT-P-20 b. The use of the SDSS-RP filter provided consistent photometry across the observed transit.
