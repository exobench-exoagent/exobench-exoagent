Scientific Report: Transit Observation of WASP-65 b

Observation Summary

The exoplanet transit of WASP-65 b was observed on 2026-03-12 using the MicroObservatory (0m4-76) telescope located at the Cerro Tololo Inter-American Observatory (Latitude: -30.1677, Longitude: -70.8047, Elevation: 2201m). The observation was conducted using a CCD camera with a pixel scale of 0.74 arcsec/pixel. The data were acquired using the SDSS-RP-119 (Sloan r) filter, with an exposure time of 60.0 seconds and a cadence of approximately 60 seconds.

Data Reduction

The raw FITS dataset was processed using the EXOTIC (v4.3.1) pipeline. Initial data cleaning included the application of bias and dark frame corrections, as well as flat-fielding, as evidenced by the FITS headers. Aperture photometry was performed with an optimal aperture of 6.67 pixels and an annulus of 9.74 pixels. Four comparison stars were identified and used for differential photometry. To account for systematic trends, the light curve was detrended using an airmass correction function with fitted coefficients of 1.218 +/- 0.01 (linear) and -0.1375 +/- 0.0094 (quadratic).

Transit Results

The transit light curve was modeled using Bayesian inference via UltraNest. The following planetary parameters were derived:

  • Mid-Transit Time (Tmid): 2461112.6033 +/- 0.0015 BJD_TDB
  • Radius Ratio (Rp/Rs): 0.1528 +/- 0.0028
  • Transit Depth: 2.33 +/- 0.087 %
  • Orbital Inclination: 86.04 +/- 0.8 degrees
  • Transit Duration: 0.1081 +/- 0.0049 days

Data Quality

The resulting light curve shows a clear transit signal with a residual scatter of 0.85%. The fit quality was high, and the derived parameters are consistent with the target's known properties, although the measured radius ratio (0.1528) is slightly higher than the prior (0.1224), which may be attributed to the specific dataset or instrumental effects.
