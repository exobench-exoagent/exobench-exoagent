EXOTIC Transit Analysis Report: HD 189733 b

Observation Summary

The target was HD 189733, observed with the Exoplanet Watch 0.4-meter telescope using a CCD camera. The observation date inferred from the FITS sequence was 2026-08-04. The data were acquired in an open/clear bandpass. The dataset contained 300 science FITS images with a measured median cadence of approximately 11 seconds per frame. Exposure time was not available in the FITS headers or EXOTIC summary.

Data Reduction

The EXOTIC Standard notebook workflow was used to load and inspect the read-only FITS dataset, identify the target, select comparison stars, generate inits.json↗, and execute EXOTIC. No dark, bias, or flat frames were present, so calibration directories were intentionally left null. Plate solving and centroid tracking were performed by EXOTIC. Aperture photometry used an optimized aperture radius of 3.77 pixels and an annulus radius of 26.77 pixels. The target and five nearby comparison-star positions were supplied to the reduction. EXOTIC calculated limb-darkening information using a custom clear-band response of approximately 350–850 nm. The light curve was detrended with airmass terms and fitted using EXOTIC’s transit-model inference routine.

Transit Results

The fitted mid-transit time was BJD_TDB 2461256.8574 ± 0.0021. The adopted orbital period was 2.218574944 ± 0.000000030 days. The fitted transit depth was 0.85 ± 1.01%, equivalent to a radius ratio of 0.092 ± 0.055. The fitted transit duration was 0.0017 ± 0.0077 days. EXOTIC reported a residual scatter of 17.77%. A formal signal-to-noise ratio, BIC, and reduced chi-squared were not provided in the generated EXOTIC outputs.

Data Quality

Although EXOTIC completed successfully, the fit is not statistically convincing. The residual scatter is very large relative to the expected HD 189733 b transit depth, the depth uncertainty exceeds the measured depth, and the duration uncertainty is substantially larger than the fitted duration. EXOTIC reported no best comparison star, indicating that the comparison ensemble did not provide a stable differential reference. The results should therefore be treated as a low-quality or non-detection transit estimate rather than a precision ephemeris measurement. No frame-level plate, flux, sky-background, or out-of-frame flags were reported for the processed sequence.
