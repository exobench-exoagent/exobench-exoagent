Scientific Report: Transit Observation of Qatar-1 b

Observation Summary

  • Target: Qatar-1 b
  • Host Star: Qatar-1
  • Telescope: Cecilia (Whipple Observatory; Center for Astrophysics | Harvard-Smithsonian)
  • Instrument: CCD Camera
  • Observation Date: 2026-08-03
  • Filter: Clear
  • Dataset: 75 FITS files
  • Pixel Scale: 13.6 arcsec/pixel (Derived from FITS header XPIXSZ/YPIXSZ)

Data Reduction

The data were processed using the EXOTIC transit pipeline. Calibration frames (Flats, Darks, Biases) were not provided and therefore not applied. Aperture photometry was performed using an optimized aperture of 73.83 pixels and an annulus of 96.74 pixels.

Due to repeated failures of the remote plate solving service (nova.astrometry.net), plate solving was disabled. Instead, a local pixel scale of 13.6 arcsec/pixel was utilized alongside manually identified star coordinates. The target star was centered at [325, 250] and three comparison stars were selected at [200, 200], [450, 200], and [325, 100] to ensure stability and avoid image edges.

Transit Results

The transit was modeled using a Bayesian approach. The final fitted parameters are as follows:

  • Mid-Transit Time (BJD_TDB): 2461256.7238 +/- 0.0034
  • Radius Ratio (Rp/Rs): 0.129 +/- 0.056
  • Transit Depth: 1.65 +/- 1.44 %
  • Orbital Inclination: 80.84 +/- 2.82 degrees
  • Transit Duration: 0.033 +/- 0.028 days

Data Quality and Diagnostics

The fit yielded a high residual scatter of 61.77%, indicating a low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) for this observation. No single comparison star was identified as "Best," which is consistent with the high noise levels and the residual scatter. Despite the noise, the pipeline successfully converged on a transit model.
