Observation Summary

The target was HAT-P-19, observed on 2026-07-11 with the MicroObservatory Cecilia telescope at Whipple Observatory. The instrument was the main CCD detector operated with 2×2 pixel binning. The observations used a clear filter, 60-second exposures, and an approximately 61.5-second cadence based on the FITS DATE-OBS and DATE-END timestamps. The dataset contained 54 science FITS images. The target coordinates used by EXOTIC were RA 00:38:03.979968 and Dec +34:42:41.049.

Data Reduction

The EXOTIC Standard notebook workflow generated a complete non-interactive inits.json↗ before execution. No separate dark, bias, or flat directories were supplied, so the reduction used the available science frames without additional calibration-frame correction. The target and three comparison stars were selected from the rendered FITS field using the AAVSO finder chart as the astrometric reference. EXOTIC performed aperture photometry, comparison-star normalization, airmass detrending, and transit-model fitting. The final reduction selected comparison star #1 at pixel coordinates [400, 432] as the best comparison.

Transit Results

The fitted mid-transit time is BJD_TDB 2461228.9923 ± 0.0043. The adopted orbital period is 4.00878322 ± 0.00000019 days, from the planetary parameter catalog prior used by EXOTIC. The fitted transit depth is 3.34 ± 0.96%, corresponding to a radius ratio of 0.183 ± 0.026. The fitted transit duration is 0.0335 ± 0.0085 days, or approximately 48.2 ± 12.2 minutes. The approximate depth-to-uncertainty signal-to-noise ratio is 3.48.

Data Quality

EXOTIC completed successfully with return code 0 and reported acceptable nested-sampling convergence. The residual scatter was approximately 2.0%. The fit included first- and second-order airmass terms of 1.043 ± 0.021 and −0.033 ± 0.037, respectively. Reduced chi-squared and BIC were not reported in the generated EXOTIC outputs. The relatively large depth and duration uncertainties, together with the approximately 2% residual scatter, indicate a moderate-confidence detection and warrant cautious interpretation.
