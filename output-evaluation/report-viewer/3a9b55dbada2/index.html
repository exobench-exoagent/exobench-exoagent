WASP-44 b Transit Observation Report

Observation Summary

The target was WASP-44, observed on 2026-07-15 with the MicroObservatory/Cecilia telescope at the Whipple Observatory. The detector was identified as a CCD operated with 2×2 pixel binning. The observation used a clear filter and 60-second exposures. Thirty-six FITS frames were processed. The time spacing between successive frames was approximately 180 seconds, corresponding to the effective cadence of the sequence. The target position was identified using the EXOTIC plate-solution workflow and matched to the catalogued coordinates of WASP-44.

Data Reduction

EXOTIC performed automated image registration, plate solving, aperture photometry, comparison-star normalization, airmass detrending, and transit-model fitting. Four comparison-star positions were supplied; EXOTIC identified Comparison 1 at pixel coordinates [250, 180] as the best comparison star. The optimized photometric aperture radius was 2.8 pixels, with an annulus parameter of 7.83 pixels. No frame-level alignment, FITS-read, low-flux, sky-background, or out-of-frame warnings were reported in the plate-status output.

Transit Results

The fitted mid-transit time is BJD_TDB 2461234.5661 ± 0.0058. The adopted orbital period is 2.42381131 ± 0.00000013 days, from the planetary-parameter catalog input. EXOTIC measured a transit depth of 2.33 ± 1.40%, equivalent to a radius ratio of 0.153 ± 0.046. The fitted transit duration is 0.027 ± 0.011 days, or approximately 38.9 ± 15.8 minutes. EXOTIC did not provide a separate SNR, BIC, or reduced chi-squared value in its final parameter summary.

Data Quality

The fit completed successfully and EXOTIC reported a valid reduction, but the residual scatter was high at 38.68%. The large uncertainty on the transit depth and duration indicates a low-precision detection. The posterior analysis also reported a positive degeneracy between inclination and mid-transit time. Consequently, these results should be treated as a preliminary photometric measurement rather than a precision ephemeris refinement.
