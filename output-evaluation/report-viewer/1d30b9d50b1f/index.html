Transit Observation Report: WASP-183 b

Observation Summary

The transit of exoplanet WASP-183 b was observed using the MicroObservatory 0.4m telescope (Telescope ID: 0m4-63) equipped with a Sony QHY600m CCD camera. Observations were conducted on 2025-03-29 using the SDSS-RP-117 (Sloan r') filter. A total of 173 FITS frames were acquired with an exposure time of 70 seconds per frame. The observatory is located at Haleakala Observatory (Lat: +20.707, Long: -156.258, Elev: 3037m).

Data Reduction

The data were processed using the EXOTIC (EXOplanet Transit Interpretation Code) pipeline. Calibration was performed using bias, dark, and flat-field frames as specified in the FITS metadata. Aperture photometry was optimized, resulting in an optimal aperture radius of 5.55 pixels and an annulus of 13.82 pixels. To account for atmospheric effects, airmass coefficients were fitted (a1 = 0.266 ± 0.03, a2 = 1.09 ± 0.046).

Transit Results

The transit was modeled using the Ultranest sampler, providing the following planetary parameters:

  • Mid-Transit Time (BJD_TDB): 2460764.813 ± 0.0061
  • Radius Ratio (Rp/Rs): 0.1862 ± 0.0083
  • Transit Depth: 3.47% ± 0.31%
  • Orbital Inclination: 85.65° ± 1.23°
  • Transit Duration: 0.045 ± 0.021 days

Data Quality

The final light curve exhibits a residual scatter of 10.58%. The fit quality is acceptable, though the relatively high scatter suggests some level of atmospheric noise or slight instability in the comparison stars. The plate solution was successfully generated, and the target star coordinates were validated against the WCS.
