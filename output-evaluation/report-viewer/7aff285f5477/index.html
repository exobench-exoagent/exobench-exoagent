Scientific Report: Exoplanet Transit Observation of KELT-9 b

Observation Summary

  • Target: KELT-9 b
  • Telescope: MicroObservatory
  • Instrument: CCD
  • Observation Date: 2023-09-24
  • Filter: MObs CV (Clear)
  • Exposure/Cadence: Standard pipeline defaults

Data Reduction

The observation dataset consisting of 300 FITS images was processed using the EXOTIC (EXOplanet Transit Interpretation Code) pipeline. The target star, KELT-9, was identified through plate solving, which corrected the initial center-of-frame coordinate guess to [257, 187]. Four comparison stars were selected based on brightness and proximity to the target.

Aperture photometry was performed using an optimal aperture of 12.52 pixels and an annulus of 31.3 pixels. Limb-darkening parameters were calculated autonomously by EXOTIC for the MObs CV filter. Detrending was applied using a second-order airmass polynomial fit to remove systematic trends.

Transit Results

The transit was fitted using a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) approach. The extracted parameters are:

  • Mid-transit time: 2460211.9551 $\pm$ 0.0034 BJD_TDB
  • Orbital period: 1.48111874 days (Fixed)
  • Transit depth: 0.17 $\pm$ 0.24 %
  • Transit duration: 0.014 $\pm$ 0.047 days
  • Orbital Inclination: 84.24 $\pm$ 2.37 degrees

Data Quality

The fit quality is relatively poor, as indicated by a residual scatter of 33.55%. The uncertainty in the transit depth (0.24%) exceeds the measured depth (0.17%), suggesting that the signal-to-noise ratio was insufficient to definitively detect the transit in this specific dataset. The high residual scatter may be attributed to atmospheric conditions or instrument noise.
