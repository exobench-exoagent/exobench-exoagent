Data Reduction

The data were processed using the EXOTIC (EXOplanet Transit Interpretation Code) pipeline. The reduction included aperture photometry of the target star (WASP-43) and four comparison stars. The target star coordinates were refined using a WCS plate solution. Detrending was performed using airmass coefficients to correct for atmospheric effects. No calibration frames (darks, flats, biases) were provided in the input dataset; therefore, the reduction relied on the normalized flux derived from the comparison stars.

Transit Results

The transit fitting was performed using an MCMC-based approach to determine the best-fit planetary parameters.

  • Mid-transit Time: $2461111.7462 \pm 0.0029$ BJD_TDB
  • Orbital Period: $0.81347407 \pm 2 imes 10^{-8}$ days
  • Transit Depth: $3.9\% \pm 2.23\%$
  • Transit Duration: $0.0611 \pm 0.0049$ days
  • Orbital Inclination: $86.79^\circ \pm 2.75^\circ$

Data Quality

The data quality for this observation is poor, characterized by a very high residual scatter of 38.0%. This high RMS indicates significant noise in the light curve, which is reflected in the large uncertainty of the transit depth \($\pm 2.23\%$\). Despite the noise, the transit mid-time was constrained with relatively high precision. The fit indicates a transit, but the high scatter suggests that the signal-to-noise ratio is low and the result should be interpreted with caution.
