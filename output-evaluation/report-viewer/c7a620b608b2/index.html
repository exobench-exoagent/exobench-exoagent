Observation Summary
The transit of exoplanet TRES-1 b was observed using the Cecilia telescope at the Whipple Observatory (Amado, AZ). Observations were conducted on 2026-08-02 using a CCD camera with 2x2 pixel binning. The data were acquired using a "Clear" (CV) filter with an exposure time of 60 seconds per frame. A total of 91 FITS images were processed.

Data Reduction
The data were reduced using the EXOTIC transit pipeline. Initial target and comparison star coordinates were refined through a plate-solve validation, which identified the target star (TRES-1) at pixel coordinates [227, 201]. Three comparison stars were selected at [192, 150], [517, 117], and [338, 352], with the star at [192, 150] identified by EXOTIC as the optimal reference. Aperture photometry was performed using an optimal aperture of 4.27 pixels and an annulus of 23.88 pixels. Detrending was applied using airmass coefficients (Am1 = 0.99 +/- 0.19, Am2 = 0.025 +/- 0.093). No calibration frames (flats, darks, or biases) were provided for this dataset.

Transit Results
The transit fit yielded a mid-transit time of BJD 2461255.8866 +/- 0.0034. The calculated radius ratio (Rp/Rs) is 0.184 +/- 0.054, corresponding to a transit depth of 3.38 +/- 1.99%. The orbital inclination was determined to be 87.4 +/- 1.43 degrees, and the transit duration was measured at 0.095 +/- 0.017 days.

Data Quality
The residual scatter for the light curve was 19.9%, indicating a relatively noisy dataset. Despite the noise, the transit signal was recovered with a mid-transit time consistent with the expected ephemeris. The fit quality was marked as "ok" by the pipeline's volume quality check.
