Scientific Report: Exoplanet Transit Observation of Qatar-10 b

Observation Summary

The transit of the exoplanet Qatar-10 b was observed using the MicroObservatory telescope on July 14, 2024. The dataset comprises 140 FITS images. Observations were captured with a CCD camera using an Open (Clear) filter, which was processed as a V-sequence (CV).

Data Reduction

The data were reduced using the EXOTIC (EXOplanet Transit Interpretation Code) pipeline. The reduction workflow included:

  • Star Identification: The target star, Qatar-10, was identified at pixel coordinates [932, 1174]. Three comparison stars were selected to produce a differential light curve.
  • Aperture Photometry: Optimal aperture (24.43 pixels) and sky annulus (48.19 pixels) were determined to maximize the signal-to-noise ratio.
  • Detrending: Atmospheric extinction and instrumental drift were corrected using airmass coefficients.
  • Transit Fitting: A Bayesian model fit was performed using the ultranest sampler to estimate the most probable planetary parameters.

Transit Results

The final fitted parameters for the transit are as follows:

  • Mid-Transit Time ($T_{mid}$): $2460506.959 \pm 0.01$ BJD_TDB
  • Orbital Period: $1.64532652$ days (Fixed)
  • Transit Depth: $2.99\% \pm 1.78\%$
  • Transit Duration: $0.108 \pm 0.01$ days
  • Orbital Inclination: $88.21^\circ \pm 2.66^\circ$
  • Radius Ratio ($R_p/R_*$): $0.173 \pm 0.051$

Data Quality

The dataset was characterized by high noise, with a residual scatter (RMS) of 26.61%. This resulted in a very low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR $\approx 0.11$), indicating that the transit signal was nearly obscured by noise. Consequently, the derived parameters have significant uncertainties.
