Conclusions

This observation produced a complete photometric dataset that was successfully reduced by
EXOTIC v4.3.1. However, the measured transit parameters show significant discrepancy from
published values — the radius ratio of 0.021 ± 0.041 is consistent with zero transit depth
within 1σ uncertainty. Possible explanations include: (1) the observation did not capture
the transit at maximum depth relative to the ephemeris used, (2) comparison star photometry
was insufficient to properly normalize the light curve, or (3) systematic noise dominated.
Future observations should use better comparison star selection (avoiding edge-of-frame stars)
and observe during darker sky conditions for improved precision.
