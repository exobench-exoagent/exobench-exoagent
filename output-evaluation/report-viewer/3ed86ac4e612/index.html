Data Quality and Assessment

  1. No transit detected: Fitted depth 0.07%±0.23% is consistent with zero (expected ~1.4%).
  2. Observation outside transit window: Mid-transit predicted at BJD 2461253.888; observation began 2.77 days later. Next transit ~BJD 2461257.43 (0.7 days after observation ended).
  3. Variable seeing: 6.9% peak-to-peak flux variation indicates atmospheric instability.
  4. Single comparison star: Limits detrending reliability; a multi-star set would improve results.
  5. One frame failed alignment: 74/75 frames usable.

Conclusions

This observation did not detect Qatar-1 b's transit because it was conducted outside the predicted transit window (2.77 days post-transit). The fitted transit parameters are not reliable measurements—they reflect the lack of constraining transit features in the data. The mid-transit time prior was carried through unchanged.

For future observations: (1) update the ephemeris from exoplanet databases, (2) observe within a 1-2 hour window of predicted mid-transit, (3) use multiple comparison stars, and (4) obtain flat-field calibration frames.
