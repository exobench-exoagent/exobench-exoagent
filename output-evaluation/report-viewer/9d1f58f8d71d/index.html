Scientific Report: Exoplanet Transit Observation of WASP-65 b

Observation Summary

Observations of the exoplanet WASP-65 b were conducted using the MicroObservatory telescope. The dataset consists of 270 FITS images acquired on March 12, 2026. The imaging was performed using a CCD camera with an open filter, effectively reducing the la-band to the V-band sequence (CV). The target star, WASP-65, was identified and centered at pixel coordinates [1177, 1148] using EXOTIC's plate-solving algorithm.

Data Reduction

The data were reduced using the EXOTIC (EXOplanet Transit Interpretation Code) pipeline. Calibration frames (darks, flats, and biases) were not available and thus omitted from the process. Aperture photometry was performed on the target and three selected comparison stars at coordinates [1451, 955], [1482, 1387], and [885, 1086]. Limb darkening coefficients were calculated automatically by EXOTIC for the standard V-band. The pipeline performed image transformation and alignment across the 270-frame sequence to produce a normalized light curve.

Transit Results

A transit model was fitted to the resulting light curve using the UltraNest sampler. The final derived planetary parameters are:

  • Mid-Transit Time: $2461112.6025 \pm 0.0014$ BJD-TDB
  • Transit Depth: $2.34\% \pm 0.087\%$ \(corresponding to a radius ratio $R_p/R_* = 0.153 \pm 0.0028$\)
  • Transit Duration: $0.1101 \pm 0.0051$ days
  • Orbital Inclination: $86.43^\circ \pm 0.88^\circ$
  • Residual Scatter: $0.84\%$

Data Quality

The reduction was successful, with a robust plate solution and effective alignment of all 270 frames. The fitting process converged well, as indicated by the UltraNest sampling efficiency and posterior uncertainty satisfaction. The residual scatter of 0.84% suggests a high-quality photometric reduction, providing a clear detection of the transit event.
