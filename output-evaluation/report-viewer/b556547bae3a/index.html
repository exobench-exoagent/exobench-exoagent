WASP-69 b Transit Observation Report

Observation Summary

The target was WASP-69, observed on 2025-09-25 with the Las Cumbres Observatory 0.4-m telescope at Cerro Tololo Inter-American Observatory. The instrument was the SQ35 CCD camera with a QHY600M detector. FITS headers identify the observing filter as Johnson V, with 12.0-second exposures. The time-series contains 300 science frames with an approximately 15.1-second cadence, including readout and overhead. The observation covered BJD\(_\mathrm{TDB}\) values near 2460943.55.

Data Reduction

EXOTIC processed the read-only FITS dataset using aperture photometry and differential photometry against three comparison stars identified from the AAVSO finder chart and the FITS WCS solution. No dark, bias, or flat frames were present, and the reduction correctly left these calibration directories absent rather than substituting the science-image directory. The optimal aperture radius was 4.27 pixels with a 10.23-pixel sky annulus. The best comparison star was the first selected comparison, AAVSO 103 at approximately pixel coordinates (427.6, 1307.9). The normalized light curve was detrended with airmass terms and fitted with a transit model. EXOTIC calculated limb-darkening parameters using the Johnson V passband.

Transit Results

The final fitted mid-transit time is BJD\(_\mathrm{TDB}\) 2460939.8806 ± 0.0061. The adopted orbital period is 3.86813881 ± 0.00000013 days. The fitted radius ratio is 0.121 ± 0.048, corresponding to a transit depth of 1.46 ± 1.16%. The reported transit duration was 0.0 ± 0.0 days, indicating that the duration was not meaningfully constrained by this reduction and should not be interpreted as a physical zero-duration transit. A nominal depth-to-residual-scatter ratio gives an approximate signal-to-noise measure of 3.65. The residual scatter was 0.4% (RMS ≈ 0.004).

Data Quality

EXOTIC completed successfully with return code 0. FITS alignment, frame validity, sky-background, low-flux, and out-of-frame checks were clear for the processed frames. The residual scatter is moderate relative to the fitted depth, and the large depth uncertainty indicates a low-significance or weakly constrained transit detection. Reduced χ² and BIC were not reported in the generated EXOTIC summaries. The unconstrained duration and broad radius-ratio uncertainty are the principal scientific limitations.
