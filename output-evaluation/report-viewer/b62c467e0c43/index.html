KELT-20 b Transit Observation Report

Observation Summary

The target was KELT-20 b, observed on 2023-08-18 with the Exoplanet Watch 0.4-meter telescope using a CCD camera. The FITS headers indicate a Pan-STARRS z-short (ZS) filter, although the generated initialization file initially described the filter as open; the EXOTIC reduction was completed using ZS. The dataset contains 300 science FITS files with 9.999-second exposures. The median cadence derived from the final time series was approximately 13.0 seconds, and the usable time span was approximately 66.2 minutes.

Data Reduction

EXOTIC v4.3.1 performed aperture photometry on the target and four comparison stars. No dark, bias, or flat calibration frames were present, so the reduction proceeded without those calibration products. A plate solution was successfully obtained using Astrometry.net. The optimal photometric aperture was 3.66 pixels with an 11.0-pixel sky annulus. Differential photometry was detrended using an airmass correction function. EXOTIC selected comparison star 1 at pixel coordinates [598, 873] as the best comparison star.

Transit Results

The fitted mid-transit time is BJD_TDB = 2460174.7017 ± 0.0011. The adopted orbital period is 3.47410042 ± 0.00000022 days. EXOTIC measured a transit depth of 0.0203 ± 0.0119, equivalent to 2.03 ± 1.19%, and a transit duration of 0.037 ± 0.017 days (approximately 53 ± 24 minutes). The fitted radius ratio was 0.143 ± 0.042. The estimated depth-to-uncertainty ratio is approximately S/N = 1.71, indicating a weak detection.

Data Quality

The final residual scatter was 0.68%, with a derived reduced chi-squared of approximately 1.77. The EXOTIC fit completed successfully and the nested-sampling diagnostics reported acceptable convergence. However, the large uncertainty on the transit depth and duration, together with the low estimated signal-to-noise ratio, means that the fitted transit parameters should be regarded as tentative rather than precision measurements. The fitted depth is also substantially uncertain relative to the published planetary prior. No BIC value was provided in the EXOTIC output.
