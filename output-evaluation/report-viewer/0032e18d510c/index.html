Data Reduction

The data were reduced using the EXOTIC pipeline. Calibration was performed using bias, dark, and flat-field frames identified in the FITS headers. Aperture photometry was implemented with an optimal aperture of 6.91 pixels and an optimal annulus of 12.09 pixels. To account for systematic variations, a first- and second-order airmass detrending was applied, with calculated coefficients $a_1 = 2.4 \pm 0.46$ and $a_2 = -0.77 \pm 1.03$.

Data Quality

The observation exhibited a residual scatter (RMS) of 19.61%, which is relatively high. Consequently, while the mid-transit time was well-localized, the transit depth and duration were not significantly detected above the noise floor. The fit results for $R_p/R_*$ \($0.009 \pm 0.047$\) are consistent with zero, suggesting that the transit signal was masked by the high noise levels in this dataset.
