Scientific Report: KELT-16 b Transit Observation

The KELT-16 b observation was reduced with EXOTIC 4.3.1 using 255 FITS science frames from the Exoplanet Watch 0.4-meter telescope dataset. The FITS headers indicate a CCD camera, 61-second exposures, 1 × 1 binning, and a V-band filter. The final EXOTIC light curve contains 253 usable photometric points spanning approximately 4.47 hours, with a median cadence of about 63.5 seconds. No Flats, Darks, or Bias frames were present, so calibration directories were left empty and no calibration frames were applied.

Aperture photometry and comparison-star detrending were performed by EXOTIC. The final fit used an optimal aperture of 4.9 pixels and an annulus radius of 10.91 pixels. EXOTIC reported no single best comparison star, indicating that the comparison-star ensemble did not produce a clearly preferred individual reference. Airmass detrending coefficients were 1.418 ± 0.014 and −0.3295 ± 0.0065.

The fitted mid-transit time is BJD-TDB 2461229.8183 ± 0.0012. The fitted radius ratio is Rp/R⋆ = 0.1454 ± 0.0010, corresponding to a transit depth of 2.11 ± 0.029%. The fitted inclination is 85.34° ± 1.85°, and the transit duration is 0.1086 ± 0.0023 days, approximately 2.61 ± 0.06 hours. The published orbital period, 0.968992962 ± 0.000000097 days, was used as the ephemeris prior rather than independently refit. EXOTIC reported residual scatter of 0.98%. Neither a formal signal-to-noise ratio, RMS metric separate from residual scatter, reduced χ², nor BIC was reported. The depth-to-residual-scatter ratio is approximately 2.2, so the transit detection should be regarded as relatively low-significance and interpreted cautiously.

The reduction required focused recovery. The first coordinate set was inconsistent with EXOTIC’s plate-solution result because the notebook-rendered image was scaled relative to the native 2400 × 2400 FITS frames. The native target coordinate suggested by the WCS cross-check was used, and plate solving was disabled in the successful run. A local pixel-scale value was supplied as a defensible reduction assumption. These configuration changes, the absence of calibration frames, the lack of a clearly preferred comparison star, and the relatively high residual scatter should be considered when using the fitted parameters.
