Signal-to-Noise Ratio

The residual scatter of 10.81% indicates significant unmodeled systematics
in the light curve. The signal-to-noise ratio for the transit detection is
low due to: (1) high photometric errors (~10% per point), (2) lack of a
valid comparison star for differential photometry, and (3) strong airmass
trends.

Data Quality Assessment

Strengths:

  • All 173 frames successfully plate-aligned with no alignment errors
  • No comparison stars went out of frame during the observation
  • Clean frame header metadata from MicroObservatory pipeline

Limitations:

  • No valid comparison star was found among the three input stars, resulting
    in differential photometry failure. This is the primary source of large
    uncertainties.

  • The fitted transit parameters have very large error bars: Rp/R* uncertainty
    is ~30%, transit depth ~60%, and duration ~89%.

  • Strong airmass trends required significant detrending.
