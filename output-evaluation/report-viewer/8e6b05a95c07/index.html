Data Reduction

The data was processed using EXOTIC v4.3.1:

  1. Plate Solving: All 50 processed images were plate-solved using astrometry.net. FITS headers contained pointing coordinates but EXOTIC performed blind plate solving.
  2. Aperture Photometry: Aperture photometry was performed on the target and five comparison stars. Optimal aperture: 4.91 pixels; optimal annulus: 8.89 pixels.
  3. Detrending: Airmass detrending with two-parameter linear model (a1 × flux + a2 × airmass).
  4. Transit Fitting: Nested sampling (UltraNest) with priors from discovery literature (Bakos et al. 2013).

Data Quality

Critical Timing Issue: The observation (starting at JD ~2460710.76) occurred approximately 19.3 hours AFTER the predicted mid-transit (2460709.95). Given the transit duration of ~3.2 hours (0.13 days), the entire observation occurred in the post-transit egress phase. This explains:

  • The fitted transit depth of 0.00024% ± 0.00093%, which is consistent with zero
  • The fitted Rp/R* of 0.0016 ± 0.003, significantly smaller than the published value (~0.00824)
  • No detectable transit signal in the light curve

Photometric Quality: The RMS of the detrended light curve is 0.78% (0.0078 relative), typical for ground-based 0.4m observations with 37% moon illumination. Single-measurement SNR of 128.4 is good.
